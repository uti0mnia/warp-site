Warp Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Warp has no server and no user accounts. The developer never receives your playlists, sign-ins or viewing activity. This policy describes what Warp stores and what it sends.

Data on your device

Playlists, guides, favorites, groups, reminders, watch history, downloads and settings are stored on your device. Passwords and keys are stored in the Keychain. Deleting Warp deletes this data.

Services you connect

Warp connects directly to the services you set up: your playlist provider and its guide, Jellyfin, Emby or Plex servers you sign in to (Plex sign-in uses plex.tv), and connectors you turn on in Settings, such as Trakt, Simkl, MDBList, OpenSubtitles, TMDB and TheIntroDB.

Each service receives only what it needs. Trakt, Simkl and MDBList receive what you watch. OpenSubtitles receives the title you request subtitles for. TMDB and TheIntroDB receive the title you are viewing. Each service can see your IP address. How they use this data is governed by their own privacy policies.

iCloud

When iCloud Sync is on, which is the default, your library is stored in your private iCloud database so your devices stay in sync. This includes playlists and their sign-ins, guides, favorites, groups, reminders, watch history and most settings. The data is stored by Apple and the developer can't access it. Playlist sign-ins are stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields. Passwords for media servers and connectors are not synced.

To disable: turn off iCloud Sync in Warp's Settings › iCloud.

To delete synced data: open the Settings app › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Warp.

Analytics

Warp sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck: app and system version, device model, crashes and freezes, and how quickly and smoothly video starts and plays. This data is used to improve Warp's performance and reliability. Each installation has a random identifier, which is hashed before it is sent. Titles, channels, addresses, accounts and anything you type are never included.

To disable: turn off Share Analytics in Warp's Settings › Help & About.

Warp Pro

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Warp receives only whether you have Warp Pro, never your name, email address or payment details.

Advertising and tracking

Warp contains no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold or shared. Warp doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Test builds

Test builds include a bug reporter. A report you send includes a screenshot and the app's recent logs. The App Store version doesn't include the bug reporter.

Children

Warp isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
