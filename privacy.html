Warp Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Warp has no server and no accounts. Your playlists, sign-ins and what you watch never reach the developer. This policy explains what Warp stores and what it sends.

Data on your device

Warp stores your playlists, guides, favorites, groups, reminders, watch history, downloads and settings on your device. Passwords and keys are kept in the Keychain. Deleting Warp deletes all of it.

Services you connect

Warp connects directly to the services you add. These include your playlist provider, any Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server you sign in to, and the connectors you turn on in Settings, such as Trakt, Simkl, MDBList, OpenSubtitles, TMDB and TheIntroDB. Signing in to Plex goes through plex.tv.

Each service gets only what it needs to work. Trakt, Simkl and MDBList see what you watch. OpenSubtitles sees the title you want subtitles for. TMDB and TheIntroDB see the title you're looking at. Every service can see your IP address, and its own privacy policy covers how it uses your data.

iCloud

iCloud Sync is on by default. It keeps your library in your private iCloud storage so it matches on all your devices. That includes playlists and their sign-ins, guides, favorites, groups, reminders, watch history and most settings. Apple stores this data, and the developer can't see it. Playlist sign-ins are encrypted. Passwords for media servers and connectors aren't synced.

To disable: turn off iCloud Sync in Warp's Settings › iCloud.

To delete synced data: in the Settings app, go to your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Warp.

Analytics

Warp sends anonymous usage data to TelemetryDeck. This includes the app and system version, the device model, crashes and freezes, and how quickly and smoothly video plays. The data is used to improve Warp's performance and reliability. Your device gets a random ID, which is hashed before it's sent. Titles, channels, addresses, accounts and anything you type are never sent.

To disable: turn off Share Analytics in Warp's Settings › Help & About.

Warp Pro

Apple handles purchases through the App Store. Warp only learns whether you have Warp Pro. It never sees your name, email address or payment details.

Advertising and tracking

Warp has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold or shared. Warp doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Test builds

Test builds have a bug reporter. If you send a report, it includes a screenshot and the app's recent logs. The App Store version doesn't have a bug reporter.

Children

Warp isn't meant for children under 13. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
